# Privacy Policy For Liberate Health Sync App

**Effective Date:** August 13, 2026

## What This App Does
This app connects to your LibreLinkUp account to retrieve your continuous
glucose monitor (CGM) readings from the last approximately 12 hours, and
writes those readings into Apple Health on your device.

## Information We Collect

**LibreLinkUp Credentials**
Your email and password for LibreLinkUp are stored securely in your device's
Keychain. They are used solely to authenticate with LibreLinkUp's servers to
retrieve your glucose data. We do not transmit, store, or have access to your
credentials on any server we control — they never leave your device except to
authenticate directly with LibreLinkUp/Abbott's own servers.

**Glucose Data**
Blood glucose readings retrieved from LibreLinkUp are written directly to
Apple Health on your device. This app does not store, transmit, sell, or
share your glucose data with any third party, advertiser, or analytics
service. We do not operate any server that receives or retains a copy of
your health data.

## Data We Do Not Collect
This app does not collect analytics, usage data, advertising identifiers, or
any personal information beyond what is described above. There are no
third-party SDKs in this app that access your health data.

## Apple HealthKit
This app uses Apple HealthKit solely to write blood glucose samples and to
check for existing entries to avoid creating duplicates. Data written to
Apple Health is governed by Apple's own Health app privacy protections, which
you control via Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

## Third-Party Services
This app communicates with LibreLinkUp/Abbott's servers to retrieve your
glucose data, using the LibreLinkUp credentials you provide. Use of
LibreLinkUp is subject to Abbott's own privacy policy and terms of service,
which are separate from this app.

## Your Choices
You can remove your stored LibreLinkUp credentials at any time from within
the app. You can revoke this app's access to Apple Health at any time via
Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → [App Name] → Turn Off All.

## Children's Privacy
This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone
under 18 without guidance from a parent, guardian, or healthcare provider.

## Changes to This Policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL
