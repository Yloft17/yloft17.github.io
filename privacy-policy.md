# Privacy Policy for Routine

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Routine ("we," "us," "our," or the "App") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our mobile application, including the main app, Apple Watch companion app, widgets, and Siri integration (collectively, the "Services").

By using Routine, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information:** Routine uses Sign in with Apple / your Apple ID (via Apple CloudKit) to create and authenticate your account. We do not create or store a separate username/password with our own servers — your identity and authentication are managed by Apple.
- **Routine and Task Data:** Routines, tasks, subtasks, notes, voice notes, checklists, and customization preferences you create within the App.
- **Workout Data:** Workouts, exercises, sets, reps, timing, and related notes you log in the App.
- **Support Communications:** Information you provide when contacting us for support, feedback, or beta testing (e.g., your email address).

### 1.2 Health and Fitness Data (Apple HealthKit)
With your explicit permission, Routine may read from and/or write to Apple HealthKit, including data such as workouts, activity, and related fitness metrics. This data is used solely to power in-app features (e.g., syncing your workouts or activity history) and:
- Is never sold.
- Is never used for advertising or marketing purposes.
- Is never shared with third parties for purposes unrelated to the requested app functionality, except as required by law.

You can revoke HealthKit permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings app under Privacy & Security > Health.

### 1.3 Information Collected Automatically
- **Analytics and Crash Data:** We use Firebase (Google) strictly for app analytics and crash reporting — to understand app performance, diagnose crashes, and improve the App. This may include device type, operating system version, app version, general usage patterns (e.g., features used, session length), and crash logs. **Firebase does not receive, store, or process your routine, task, workout, or health data** — that data is stored and synced exclusively through Apple CloudKit, described in Section 1.1 and Section 4 below.
- **Device and Log Data:** IP address, device identifiers, and other diagnostic information may be collected automatically by Firebase.

### 1.4 Information from Apple Watch, Widgets, and Siri
- **Apple Watch App:** Syncs routine and workout data between your iPhone and Apple Watch to keep your progress consistent across devices.
- **Widgets:** Displays a summary of your routines/tasks on your home or lock screen; this data is read from your existing account/local data and is not separately collected.
- **Siri Integration:** If you use Siri Shortcuts with Routine, Apple may process your voice request to trigger actions within the App. We do not receive or store your voice recordings; Apple's Siri privacy practices govern that processing.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your account
- Provide, operate, and sync your routines and workout data across your devices
- Sync data with Apple HealthKit (with your permission)
- Diagnose bugs, crashes, and performance issues
- Understand feature usage to improve the App
- Respond to support requests and beta tester communications
- Maintain the security and integrity of the App
- Comply with legal obligations

We do **not** sell your personal information.

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

- **Apple (CloudKit):** Your routine, task, workout, and account data is stored and synced through Apple CloudKit, within your personal iCloud account. This data is scoped to our App's private container and is not accessible to other apps or to us outside of what's needed to provide the Service. Apple also provides HealthKit sync, Sign in with Apple authentication, Apple Watch functionality, and Siri Shortcuts.
- **Firebase (Google) — Analytics and Crash Reporting Only:** We use Firebase solely for app analytics and crash diagnostics, as described in Section 1.3. Firebase does not receive or store your routine, workout, or health data — only device/usage diagnostics. Google is contractually and technically limited to providing this diagnostic service.
- **Legal Requirements:** If required to do so by law, or in response to valid legal requests by public authorities.
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy or a materially similar one.

We do not share your health or fitness data with advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Routine and workout data is stored via Apple CloudKit, within your personal iCloud account, and syncs automatically across your devices signed in with the same Apple ID (iPhone, Apple Watch, etc.). We do not operate a separate database of your routine or workout content.
- Because this data resides in your own iCloud account, Apple's iCloud security and encryption protections apply. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how iCloud data is protected.
- Diagnostic data (analytics/crash reports) is separately transmitted to Firebase, as described above.
- We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
- No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

- **Account Data:** You may update or delete your account information within the App, or by contacting us.
- **HealthKit Permissions:** You can grant or revoke HealthKit access at any time via iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Routine.
- **Analytics Opt-Out:** Depending on your device settings, you may be able to limit ad tracking and analytics collection via your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security.
- **Data Deletion:** You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.
- **Regional Privacy Rights:** Depending on your location (e.g., California, EU/UK), you may have additional rights, such as the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and the right to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.
